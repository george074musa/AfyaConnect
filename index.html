Nutrition Awareness

🥬

Vegetables and Fruits

Provide vitamins, minerals and fibre that support good health.

🌽

Energy Foods

Foods such as maize, rice, potatoes and other grains provide energy.

🥚

Protein Foods

Beans, eggs, fish and other protein sources support growth and body maintenance.

🥛

Calcium-Rich Foods

Milk and other calcium sources help support healthy bones and teeth.

💧

Water

Safe drinking water supports hydration and normal body functions.

🍽️

Balanced Meals

Include a variety of foods from different food groups.

KEEP YOUR COMMUNITY HEALTHY

Personal and Environmental Hygiene

Simple practices that help protect our communities.

🧼

Handwashing

Wash your hands with soap and clean running water at important times, including before eating and after using the toilet.

🦷

Oral Hygiene

Brush your teeth regularly and seek dental check-ups when needed.

🚰

Safe Water

Use safe drinking water and store it in clean, covered containers.

🗑️

Waste Management

Dispose of rubbish responsibly and keep public spaces clean.

🏠

Clean Homes

Keep living spaces clean and well ventilated.

🌳

Clean Environment

Participate in community clean-up activities and protect shared spaces.
